# CoachPro
CoachPro is a modern coaching centre management system for managing students, courses, attendance, fees, and reports from one centralized dashboard.
CoachPro — Description

CoachPro is a modern Coaching Centre Management SaaS designed to simplify the daily management of coaching institutes. It helps coaching owners and administrators manage students, courses, attendance, fees, reports, and centre settings from one easy-to-use dashboard.

Key Features
👨‍🎓 Student Management — Add, update, search and manage student records.
📚 Course Management — Manage courses such as Spoken English and Pro Computer.
📅 Attendance Management — Record daily attendance and view student-wise/monthly reports.
💰 Fee Management — Track monthly fees, paid/pending amounts and payment history.
📊 Reports & Dashboard — Get a quick overview of students, attendance and fees.
⚙️ Settings — Manage coaching centre information and application data.
🔐 SaaS-ready Architecture — Designed to evolve into a multi-coaching platform where each coaching centre can securely manage its own data.
